# Bangladesh national monthly temperature and rainfall, 1901-2023

- Files: `bgd_monthly_temp_rain_1901_2023.csv` (year, month, temp_c = monthly mean temperature in C, rain_mm = monthly total rainfall in mm; 1,474 rows), `bgd_annual_temp_rain_1901_2023.csv` (123 rows; annual mean temperature, annual rainfall total set to NaN when fewer than 12 rain months), `REPAIR_LOG.txt`.
- Repo: https://github.com/nomanabdullah26/Climate-Data-Analysis (commit bd2271a, 2026-09-27), file `sorted_temp_and_rain_dataset.csv`. No licence file in the repo; data are a re-host.
- Original source: this is the widely circulated Kaggle series "Bangladesh Weather Dataset / Temp_and_rain.csv" (header `tem,Month,Year,rain`, first row `16.976,1,1901,18.5356`), whose 1901-2015 part was taken from the World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal country download (CRU TS gridded data, country-area average for Bangladesh). The 2016-2023 extension comes from the newer Kaggle re-upload used by this repo; its uploader and method could not be verified from GitHub, so treat 2016-2023 as unverified. Values are country-area averages, not station observations.
- Licence: World Bank CCKP data are CC BY 4.0; CRU TS is Open Government Licence. The Kaggle re-host and GitHub mirror give no explicit licence.
- What was done: dropped the junk `name` column; the repo copy is a "data cleaning exercise" with planted errors, which were repaired as listed in REPAIR_LOG.txt (one row with a missing year and one with a missing month filled from their neighbours; 1904-11 temp 2.334, 1916-07 temp 7.0 and 1916-01 rain 25000 set to NaN). Annual aggregates computed with pandas. 2023 has only Jan-Oct.
- Quality warning (found at verification): annual means for 2012-2022 run about 0.6 C below 2005-2011 (24.5-25.4 vs 25.5-26.2 C) while the BMD station series keeps warming through 2013 and the BMD 24-station series gives 26.6-26.7 C for 2021-2022. The post-2011 part of this series therefore looks like a splice of a different source and should not be used for the temperature trend after 2011; use it for 1901-2011 (CRU/CCKP lineage) and switch to `climate_bmd_stations_1948_2013` and `climate_bmd_national_annual_1982_2022` for recent decades. The rainfall totals do not show a comparable step.
