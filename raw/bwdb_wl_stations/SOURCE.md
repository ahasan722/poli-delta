# BWDB water level stations

- `stations.geojson`: 357 Bangladesh Water Development Board surface water level stations with station ID, name, river, district, upazila and coordinates, as published in the open repository MdManzurRahman/mdmanzurrahman.github.io (`gisdata/data.json`). Used only to place the FFWC danger-level stations on the map, matched by station name.
