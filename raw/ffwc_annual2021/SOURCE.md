# FFWC Annual Flood Report 2021

- Source: Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC), Bangladesh Water Development Board, *Annual Flood Report 2021*, https://api.ffwc.gov.bd/assets/annual-reports/annual21.pdf
- `danger_levels.csv`: the report's water level tables (Tables 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4): station, river, danger level, previously recorded maximum water level and the 2021 peak, all in metres above PWD datum. Read from the report's text; the South Eastern Hill stations and the dated record table are not included. The report's table gives the Teesta at Dalia danger level as 52.60 m while its text gives 52.40 m; the table value is kept.
- `flood_extent.csv`: the report's table of flood-affected area by year, 1954-2021 (km² and % of the country). Years missing from the table are missing here.
