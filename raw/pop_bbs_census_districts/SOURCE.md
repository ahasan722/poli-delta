# District population, BBS census 2022 and 2011

- `bbs_districts_population_2011_2022.csv` (64 rows). Columns: geo_code, division_code, division_name, district_code, district_name, pop_2022_bbs_gis (BBS 2022 census GIS layer count, see adm2_districts/SOURCE.md), total_male, total_female, sex_ratio, hh_size, enumerated_hh, total_nrb, area_km2_geom (from the BBS polygon, EPSG:6933), pop_density_km2, wiki_name, pop_2011 (BBS 2011 census), pop_2022_wikipedia (BBS 2022 preliminary report figure), area_km2_wikipedia, growth_2011_2022_pct (pop_2022_bbs_gis vs pop_2011).
  - Sources: 2022 counts and geometry from https://github.com/justinelliotmeyers/bangladesh_2022_census_gis `district.gpkg` (BBS census GIS portal). 2011 counts, 2022 preliminary-report counts and official areas from the earlier work file `/home/claude/work/data/raw/population_admin.csv` (compiled from Wikipedia "Districts of Bangladesh" tables, which transcribe the BBS 2011 and 2022 census reports). All 64 districts matched after spelling aliases (Chittagong/Chattogram, Comilla/Cumilla, Barisal/Barishal, Jessore/Jashore, Bogra/Bogura, Chapai Nawabganj/Chapainababganj, Jhalokathi/Jhalokati, Netrokona/Netrakona). Sums: 2011 = 144,189,225; 2022 GIS = 165,498,422.
- `census2022_district_indicators_literacy_sexratio_electricity.csv` (64 rows: district, literacy_rate_7plus, sex_ratio, electricity_coverage) from https://github.com/Ishrat2710/bangladesh-census2022-district-indicators (commit db1231d), compiled from BBS Census 2022 district tables P11, P14, HH12. No licence file.
- Licence: BBS census tables are public government statistics; the GitHub re-hosts carry no explicit licence.
- What was done: attribute join with pandas, area computed with geopandas.
