# BMD station monthly climate, 33 stations, 1948-2013

- Files: `bmd_station_monthly_1948_2013.csv` (20,544 rows: station, wmo_id, year, month, tmax_c, tmin_c = monthly mean daily max/min temperature C, rain_mm = monthly total rainfall mm, rh_pct, wind_ms, cloud_okta, sunshine_h, flood_flag = 1 when the compiler recorded a flood in that station's area that month, 0 = explicitly no flood, blank = not assessed), `bmd_station_annual_1948_2013.csv` (1,712 station-years; rain total NaN if <12 months; tmean_c = (tmax+tmin)/2; flood_months count), `bmd_national_mean_of_stations_annual.csv` (66 years, unweighted mean of complete station-years, n_stations given), `bmd_stations.csv` (33 stations with WMO id, lat, lon, altitude m, record span).
- Repo: https://github.com/n-gauhar/Flood-prediction (commit 99753ba, 2022-10-06), file `FloodPrediction.csv`. Dataset of Gauhar, Das and Moury (2021), "Prediction of Flood in Bangladesh using k-Nearest Neighbors Algorithm", ICREST 2021 (IEEE). Cite that paper if used.
- Original source: Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) monthly station records as re-hosted on Kaggle by Emon Reza, "65 years of weather data Bangladesh (preprocessed)" (https://www.kaggle.com/emonreza/65-years-of-weather-data-bangladesh-preprocessed); flood flags compiled by the authors from FFWC/BWDB annual flood reports, ReliefWeb, Banglapedia, Daily Star and Dartmouth Flood Observatory (list in the repo README).
- Licence: none stated in the repo; BMD data are government records normally released on request. Treat as research use with citation.
- What was done: renamed columns, verified units (annual station totals average 2,385 mm, so rainfall is mm, not cm as the README says), dropped the projected X/Y columns and the `Period` decimal date, computed station annual and national mean files with pandas. Stations: 31 BMD synoptic stations plus two Chittagong gauges; Chittagong (City-Ambagan) only 2009-2013.
