# National population series
- wdi_pop: World Bank WDI SP.POP.TOTL (source 2), 1960-2025, api.worldbank.org, fetched 25 Nov 2026.
- wb_proj: World Bank Population Estimates and Projections (source 40), SP.POP.TOTL 2025-2050, last updated 2026-07-01.
- tfr: WDI SP.DYN.TFRT.IN, selected years.
- census: BBS census counts and BBS figures adjusted for net under-enumeration [year, enumerated, adjusted], via Wikipedia "Demographics of Bangladesh" census table; 2011 adjusted is the BBS official 149,772,364.
