# PSMSL tide-gauge mean sea level, northern Bay of Bengal

- Files: `psmsl_rlr_annual.csv` (262 rows: PSMSL annual RLR means in mm, 7000 mm offset kept in rlr_mm and removed in rlr_minus_7000_mm; flag_attention Y/N; missing_flag), `psmsl_rlr_monthly.csv` (3,068 monthly RLR values from the repo author's compiled dataframe, decimal_year, rsl_mm already offset-corrected, attention flags), `psmsl_annual_from_monthly.csv` (annual means recomputed from the monthly values with n_months, longer than the official annual file because PSMSL drops any year with a missing month), `psmsl_stations.csv` (8 gauges: Hiron Point 1451, Khepupara 1454, Cox's Bazar 1476, Charchanga 1496, Chittagong A 2196, and Indian Hooghly-estuary gauges Sagar 417, Diamond Harbour 543, Haldia 1270, with lat/lon and PSMSL coastline/station codes).
- Coverage: Hiron Point 1983-2003, Khepupara 1977-2000, Cox's Bazar 1978-2000, Charchanga 1979-2000, Chittagong A 2007-2018 (new UHSLC gauge, cannot be joined to older Chittagong data), Diamond Harbour 1948-2015, Haldia 1970-2015, Sagar 1937-1988. PSMSL holds no Bangladesh data after 2003 except Chittagong A, so the Bangladesh gauges do not reach the 2020s.
- Repo: https://github.com/maeveupton/tide_gauge_data_PSMSL (commit b159811, 2023-08-10), a snapshot of the PSMSL RLR annual download (`rlr_annual/data/*.rlrdata`, `filelist.txt`, `docu/`) dated 29 Nov 2021 plus the author's monthly compilation `output_dataframes/annual_monthly_SL_tide_df.csv`. No licence file in the repo.
- Original source: Permanent Service for Mean Sea Level (PSMSL), Holgate et al. 2013, J. Coastal Res. 29(3), 493-504, https://psmsl.org/data/obtaining/. Supplying authority for the Bangladesh gauges: Bangladesh Inland Water Transport Authority, Department of Hydrography (via TOGA / UHSLC). PSMSL data are free to use with acknowledgement of PSMSL and citation of Holgate et al. 2013.
- Caveats from PSMSL station docs: Cox's Bazar reference level appears about 20 cm low from Apr 1994; Charchanga RLR revised 2013; Bangladesh gauges sit on subsiding delta ground, so RLR trends include vertical land motion.
- What was done: parsed the semicolon rlrdata files, filtered the author's monthly dataframe to these 8 ids, computed annual means from monthly values with pandas.
