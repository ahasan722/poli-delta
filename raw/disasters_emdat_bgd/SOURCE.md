# EM-DAT disasters for Bangladesh, 1900-2026, and a named-cyclone table

- `emdat_bgd_natural_disasters_1900_2026.csv` (373 rows, all EM-DAT natural-hazard records with ISO BGD: 190 storms, 104 floods, 32 epidemics, 25 extreme temperature, 10 earthquakes, 6 droughts, 6 wet mass movements; 1904-2026). Columns: disno, disaster_type, disaster_subtype, event_name, location, origin, associated_types, magnitude (km/h for storms), magnitude_scale, latitude, longitude, river_basin, start/end year-month-day, total_deaths, no_injured, no_affected, no_homeless, total_affected, total_damage_000_us (thousand US$ nominal), total_damage_adjusted_000_us (CPI-adjusted), cpi, admin_units (JSON of GAUL/GADM units), entry_date, last_update.
- `emdat_bgd_storm_flood_by_year.csv` (132 rows): events, deaths, affected and nominal damage summed per start_year and type (Storm / Flood). Gives the flood-year list (104 EM-DAT floods 1960-2025) and the annual count of damaging storms.
- `named_cyclones_bgd_1970_2024.csv` (18 rows): Bhola 1970, Urir Char 1985, 04B 1988, Gorky 1991, 01B 1997, Sidr 2007, Aila 2009, Mahasen 2013, Roanu 2016, Mora 2017, Fani 2019, Bulbul 2019, Amphan 2020, Yaas 2021, Sitrang 2022, Mocha 2023, Hamoon 2023, Remal 2024. Deaths, total_affected, damage (nominal and adjusted, thousand US$), EM-DAT location string and wind magnitude come from the EM-DAT record named in `impact_source`. The columns `landfall_date`, `landfall_district` and `surge_height_m` are NOT from a downloaded file: they were typed in from the Wikipedia article named in `landfall_surge_source` (which in turn cites BMD, IMD and JTWC reports) and are approximate ranges; verify before publishing. Note EM-DAT figures differ from Bangladesh government counts for some events (e.g. Sidr 4,234 vs GoB 3,406 deaths; Remal 16 vs 19; Amphan damage 1.5 bn US$ vs GoB ~130 m US$).
- Repo: https://github.com/Biblejustin/flood-data (commit c68e743, 2026-09-09, MIT for the repo), file `emdat_all_disasters_2026-05-18.xlsx` = EM-DAT public table `public_emdat_incl_hist`, version 2026-05-15, 27,640 records, sheet "EM-DAT Data".
- Original source: EM-DAT, CRED / UCLouvain, Brussels, Belgium, www.emdat.be. EM-DAT terms: free for non-commercial use with the citation "EM-DAT, CRED / UCLouvain, Brussels, Belgium - www.emdat.be"; redistribution of the full database is not permitted, which is why only the Bangladesh extract is kept here.
- Also checked (2000+ only, superseded): makiroll1125/global-disaster-risk-explorer (EM-DAT custom request 2026-09-15) and masonphung/global_disaster_dashboard (public_emdat_20240923.xlsx); their Bangladesh storm rows match this file.
- What was done: filtered with pandas, renamed columns to snake_case, built the per-year and named-cyclone tables.
